## Evaluation Principles Adhered To

1. **Held-out testing:** Reported performance is computed solely on test/validation data never seen during gradient updates.
2. **Deterministic metrics:** Confusion matrices, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, and Brier scores computed via scikit-learn.
3. **No fabrication:** Exact values generated by `ml/mobilenetv3/training/train.py` and `ml/xgboost/training/train.py`.
